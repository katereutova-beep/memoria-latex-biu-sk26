# PRD — Memoria: Business Plan & Price Quote (LaTeX, LuaLaTeX)

Group code: `biu-sk26` · Assignment 02 · Course L07 (Dr. Yoram Segal)

## 1. Vision
A publisher-grade, bilingual (Hebrew RTL + English LTR) PDF that presents **Memoria**,
a studio that restores and digitally archives family photographs with AI. The document is
at once a business plan and a detailed price quote. It must read as a document a real
client or investor could act on, and the build must be fully reproducible from source.

Topic differs from typical submissions: a *service business* with real unit economics,
a tiered quote, and a risk model, not a tutorial or a product demo.

## 2. Expert Committee (simulated review panel)
| Expert | Mandate | Verdict (criteria set) |
|---|---|---|
| Typography / BiDi lead | Hebrew+English mixing, fonts, numbers, punctuation | Every English term in a Hebrew paragraph wrapped by `\en{}`; numbers and URLs LTR; no reversed parentheses; one font family covering both scripts |
| Business analyst | Credibility of plan and quote | All figures derived from a single Python model (`code/model.py`); assumptions listed; break-even shown |
| Visual designer | Identity, not defaults | Own palette (deep teal + amber, sepia accent), banner + footer, TikZ-only illustrations, no external images |
| LaTeX engineer | Reproducibility | `main.tex` + one `.tex` per chapter, custom `.cls`, `lualatex` → `biber` → `lualatex` ×2 builds clean |
| QA lead | Zero visible defects | Detector/Fixer loop; red-team review; no overfull boxes, no empty pages, no orphan headings |
| Process auditor | Engineering dialogue | Numbered prompts in `prompts/`, skills in `skills/`, incident post-mortems, frequent commits by both partners |

## 3. Functional requirements (minimum, from assignment)
- FR1 Length ≥ 15 pages, Hebrew + English mixed (target 18–22).
- FR2 Contains images/figures, graphs, summaries, tables, text.
- FR3 Chapters, table of contents, IEEE-style bibliography (biblatex-ieee, biber).
- FR4 Custom `.cls`; Footer + Banner (excellence).
- FR5 Correct BiDi, Hebrew and English.
- FR6 ≥ 1 image, ≥ 1 table, ≥ 1 math formula (excellence), all present.

## 4. Quality requirements (excellence)
- Graphs are generated by Python (matplotlib) from `code/model.py`, exported as vector PDF with Hebrew-capable fonts.
- Illustrations are TikZ (before/after restoration pipeline, org chart, timeline).
- Iron rule: **never fix text directly**. Every fix passes through a Skill or CLS update; the build is re-run.
- Skills: `qa-super`, `qa-BiDi`, `qa-table`, `qa-code`, `qa-img`, `qa-typeset`, `qa-infra`, `qa-cls-version`, plus `detector` and `fixer` and a `red-team` pass.

## 5. Out of scope
Photographs from the internet; Word/PDF-editor post-processing; hand edits of the PDF.

## 6. Acceptance criteria
1. `lualatex main.tex && biber main && lualatex main.tex && lualatex main.tex` succeeds from a clean clone.
2. `qa-super` reports zero blocking findings; the incident log lists every defect found by humans with root cause and why it was missed.
3. Repository contains source, class, figures, Python, final PDF, prompts, skills, README with screenshots, PRD/PLAN/TODO.
4. Self-assessed grade is chosen honestly after the final red-team round (recorded in `docs/self-assessment.md`).

## 7. Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Hebrew glyphs missing in a font | Single OpenType family (Libertinus) for Hebrew+Latin; `qa-BiDi` greps the log for missing-glyph warnings |
| Matplotlib Hebrew rendering | Use RTL-aware text helper, verify visually via `qa-img` |
| Token/usage limits with many agents | Cheaper model for Detector, stronger for Writer; commit after every stage |
