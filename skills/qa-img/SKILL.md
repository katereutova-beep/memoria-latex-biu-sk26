# qa-img
- Every figure is vector (PDF/TikZ) or >=200 dpi; has a caption and a `\label`, referenced in text.
- No clipped labels, no text over shapes, legible at print size (>=7pt).
- Colors follow the Memoria palette; colorblind-safe pairs (teal/amber, not red/green).
- No external or copyrighted images: all art is TikZ or generated by `code/`.
- Figures do not float more than one page from their reference.
